Photo: そらみみ / CC BY-SA 4.0 (Wikimedia Commons)

"Your Name" Anime Pilgrimage Guide

Photo: そらみみ / CC BY-SA 4.0 (Wikimedia Commons)

Director Makoto Shinkai's film "Your Name" is set in the fictional town of Itomori, modeled on real locations in Hida City, Gifu Prefecture, weaving a story that connects Tokyo and rural Japan. Beyond real spots in the Takayama area, such as Hida-Furukawa Station and Keta Wakamiya Shrine, fans also visit Tokyo locations including the KITTE Marunouchi rooftop garden and the Shinanomachi pedestrian bridge, the setting for one of the film's most memorable scenes.

Pilgrimage Tips

Since the Gifu and Tokyo locations are far apart, we recommend planning a multi-day itinerary to visit both. Local trains in the Hida area run infrequently, so check the timetable in advance.
