所要時間の目安

全スポットが大洗町内に集中しており、徒歩・レンタサイクルで半日程度で回れます。

巡礼のヒント

大洗は東京から特急で日帰りも可能な距離です。商店街のお店では『ガールズ&パンツァー』とのコラボメニューやグッズも楽しめます。

よくある質問

『ガールズ&パンツァー』の聖地はどこにありますか？

聖地はすべて茨城県大洗町にあります。大洗磯前神社、大洗マリンタワー、大洗海岸、大洗の商店街一帯など、実際に訪れられるスポットが集まっています。

聖地巡礼にはどのくらい時間がかかりますか？

全スポットが大洗町内に集中しており、徒歩・レンタサイクルで半日程度で回れます。

"Girls und Panzer" Anime Pilgrimage Guide

Photo: Saigen Jiro / Public Domain (Wikimedia Commons)

Oarai, a port town in Ibaraki Prefecture, is widely known among fans at home and abroad as the setting for the TV anime "Girls und Panzer." The torii gate of Oarai Isosaki Shrine, fan-decorated votive plaques along the shopping street, and Oarai Beach all remain much as they appear in the series, making it a classic pilgrimage destination.

Estimated Time Needed

All spots are within Oarai town, walkable or reachable by rental bicycle in about half a day.

Pilgrimage Tips

Oarai is close enough to Tokyo for a day trip by limited express train. Many shops along the shopping street also offer "Girls und Panzer" collaboration menus and merchandise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the "Girls und Panzer" pilgrimage spots located?

All the pilgrimage spots are in Oarai, a port town in Ibaraki Prefecture, including Oarai Isosaki Shrine, Oarai Marine Tower, Oarai Beach, and the Oarai Shopping Street Area — all real places you can visit today.

How long does the pilgrimage take?

All the spots are within Oarai town, so you can cover them on foot or by rental bicycle in about half a day.

Questions fréquentes

Où se trouvent les lieux de pèlerinage de "Girls und Panzer" ?

Tous les lieux de pèlerinage se trouvent à Oarai, une ville portuaire de la préfecture d'Ibaraki, notamment Oarai Isosaki Shrine, Oarai Marine Tower, Oarai Beach et Oarai Shopping Street Area — tous des lieux réels que l'on peut visiter aujourd'hui.

Combien de temps dure le pèlerinage ?

Tous les sites se trouvent dans la ville d'Oarai, on peut donc les parcourir à pied ou à vélo de location en une demi-journée environ.
